# Contributing to ENZO V1 Free

V1 Free is a stable reference baseline. Corrections are welcome; features and experiments belong in a separate variant or the appropriate later tier.

## Build questions and progress

[Building ENZO / show your build](https://github.com/icu6t6/DevilsLAB/issues/new?template=build-report.yml). Parts questions, partial builds and finished builds are all welcome. You can also open a normal issue for a focused question.

## Proposing a correction

Identify the affected file and build stage, what you observed, what you expected, and your proposed correction. Include the source revision and substituted hardware where relevant. Distinguish a documentation error from a change to physical wiring or firmware behaviour.

Discuss changes before preparing a large pull request. Preserve the known-good pin map and canonical firmware. Do not add OLED, DHT, telemetry, diagnostics UI or experimental features to Free V1. Diagnostics belong in the appropriate Advanced tier. Deliberate baseline revisions require owner review.

## Material you submit

Share only material you have permission to share. Keep Wi-Fi passwords, tokens and personal details out of logs, code and photos. Build-report photos are optional and remain the contributor’s material; posting a report does not grant commercial reuse permission for its photos.

Proposed source or documentation contributions should identify their licence. The owner must resolve any additional permissions needed for future commercial reuse before accepting such material; do not assume that submitting a correction transfers copyright or commercial rights.

The [V1 licence](LICENSE) governs the listed repository material. Public availability does not grant permission to reuse excluded later-tier material.
